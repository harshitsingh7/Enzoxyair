## Brand & Style

This design system embodies atmospheric architectural minimalism and high-spec environmental wellness. Designed for a discerning audience that views air purity, biophilic living, and environmental balance as essential forms of modern luxury, the aesthetic balances scientific rigor with sensory calm.

The visual direction marries high-end Scandinavian industrial product design with Swiss editorial restraint. Spatial composition relies on expansive breathing room, monolithic uppercase typography, whisper-thin hairline dividing grids, and tactile glass surfaces. The emotional tone evokes pure alpine atmosphere, medical-grade confidence, and effortless residential elegance. Visual noise is eradicated in favor of deliberate pacing, muted textural layers, and precise electric micro-accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an interplay between dark oceanic mass and sterile atmospheric lightness.

- **Primary (`#0B1919`)**: Deep Teal-Black anchors the interface. It serves as the primary canvas for high-impact editorial sections, dark lifestyle hero units, and the standard high-contrast text color on light ground.
- **Secondary (`#2DE2BA`)**: Electric Aqua operates as a calibrated energetic signal. Used strictly for critical call-to-actions, live sensor feedback, filtration activity indicators, and focal data highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D4E5E1`)**: Soft Sage/Aqua Tint acts as a soothing transitional surface tier, container fill, and secondary interactive state.
- **Neutral (`#F7F9F8`)**: Soft Off-White provides a glare-free, organic foundation for primary reading surfaces, preventing the clinical sterile fatigue of raw white.

Complementary surfaces use **Mist Grey (`#E8EDED`)** for low-contrast structural dividing planes and card wells, while **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)** is reserved for raised active cards and backlit metric readouts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural cadence across technical data and narrative storytelling.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Rendered in **Space Grotesk**, set primarily in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`). This delivers a sculptural, precision-engineered character akin to luxury technical chronometers and art monographs.
- **Body & Continuous Reading**: Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** for soft, balanced humanism, offering effortless legibility across clinical white papers, product specs, and narrative lifestyle features.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Fixed in uppercase **Space Grotesk** with wide tracking (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to denote environmental telemetry, sensor statuses, and categorized navigation paths.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined 12-column architectural grid grounded in generous negative space.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and a minimum `4rem` outer canvas margin. Sections utilize unhurried vertical rhythm with `space-xl` and double-scaled margins to allow hardware photography and diagnostic diagrams room to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Multicolumn spec cards collapse into structured 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Typography scales automatically to mobile tokens to maintain visual hierarchy without line wrap clumping.

Horizontal rules are treated as razor-thin structural axes (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 25, 0.08)`), anchoring content blocks without visual weight.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through atmospheric tonal layering and soft diffusion rather than aggressive physical drop shadows.

- **Base Layer**: The neutral foundation (`#F7F9F8`) hosts content modules.
- **Sub-surface Insets**: Recessed telemetry monitors and quiet containers leverage `#E8EDED` with flush borders.
- **Floating Cards**: Cards resting on light ground rely on pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) framed by an ultra-faint hairline border (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 25, 0.06)`). When elevated, they cast an ultra-wide, low-density ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Dark Mode / Cinematic Modules**: On `#0B1919` canvases, depth is rendered via frosted acrylic overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), backdropped by a `20px` Gaussian blur and edged with a subtle light catch line (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a geometric contrast between fluid, pill-shaped tactile controls and structured architectural card containers.

- **Primary Interactive Elements**: Buttons, search bars, active filter pills, and alert badges feature a full radius (`9999px`) to invite natural physical touch and echo air molecular purity.
- **Card Containers & Panels**: Use the `roundedness: 2` scale (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` on `rounded-lg`, and `1.5rem` on `rounded-xl`). Large hero panels and imagery display cards employ `1.5rem` rounded corners to soften spatial transitions while preserving architectural geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill shape (`9999px`), `#0B1919` surface with `#FFFFFF` or `#2DE2BA` Space Grotesk label in uppercase. On hover, background shifts to a radiant `#122525` with subtle micro-scale expansion (`scale(1.015)`).
- **Accent (Live Action)**: `#2DE2BA` fill with `#0B1919` text, used exclusively for primary conversion and active operation controls.
- **Ghost/Outline**: Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(11, 25, 25, 0.2)` border, smooth transition to `#0B1919` text on `#D4E5E1` surface fill on hover.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact pill height with wide-tracked `label-md` uppercase text.
- Inactive state: `#E8EDED` fill with `#0B1919` text.
- Active state: `#0B1919` fill with `#2DE2BA` text or glowing secondary bullet indicator.

### Cards
- **Product & Spec Cards**: `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-xl` corners, hairline borders (`rgba(11, 25, 25, 0.06)`). Padding utilizes `space-lg`. Imagery floats edge-to-edge or with generous internal whitespace.
- **Metric Cards (AQI & Sensor Data)**: Deep teal variant (`#122525`) on dark layouts with blurred frosted overlays, displaying oversized metric numerals and an animated `#2DE2BA` status pulse.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Generous ergonomic height (52px), rounded pill or soft rounded (`0.75rem`) container with `#FFFFFF` fill and a recessed inner border. Focused state highlights with a sharp `1.5px solid #2DE2BA` outline and zero intrusive drop shadows.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are circular rings with a concentrated `#2DE2BA` center core. Checkboxes maintain soft 4px radius edges, filling with `#0B1919` and displaying a crisp `#2DE2BA` indicator glyph when selected.

### Environmental Sensor Gauges & Dials
- Minimalist circular radial sweeps composed of hairline track arcs. Current particulate and air health readings (PM2.5, VOC, Humidity) are rendered in large numeric figures with uppercase microscopic labels underneath.
